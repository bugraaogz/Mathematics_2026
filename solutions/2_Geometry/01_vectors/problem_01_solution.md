## Question

For $u=(2,-1,3)$ and $v=(-1,4,2)$ compute $u+v$, $u-v$, and $3u-2v$. Verify that $(u+v)-v=u$, and explain geometrically what adding two vectors means.

## Solution

Vector operations are performed component by component:

$$u+v = (2+(-1), -1+4, 3+2) = (1, 3, 5)$$

$$u-v = (2-(-1), -1-4, 3-2) = (3, -5, 1)$$

$$3u-2v = 3(2,-1,3) - 2(-1,4,2) = (6,-3,9) - (-2,8,4) = (8, -11, 5)$$

Verification of $(u+v)-v = u$:
$$(u+v)-v = (1,3,5) - (-1,4,2) = (1-(-1), 3-4, 5-2) = (2,-1,3) = u$$

Geometrically, adding vectors $u$ and $v$ corresponds to the head-to-tail rule: placing the tail of vector $v$ at the head of vector $u$. The resulting sum vector $u+v$ represents the total displacement from the initial tail of $u$ to the final head of $v$ (or the main diagonal of the parallelogram formed by $u$ and $v$).
