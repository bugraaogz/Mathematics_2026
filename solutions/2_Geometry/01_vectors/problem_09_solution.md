## Question

For $u=(1,2,0)$ and $v=(0,1,3)$ compute $u\times v$. Use dot products to check perpendicularity to both vectors, compute $v\times u$, and compare the results.

## Solution

1. Cross product $u \times v$:
$$u \times v = (2\cdot 3 - 0\cdot 1,\, 0\cdot 0 - 1\cdot 3,\, 1\cdot 1 - 2\cdot 0) = (6, -3, 1)$$

2. Perpendicularity checks:
$$(u \times v) \cdot u = 6(1) + (-3)(2) + 1(0) = 6 - 6 + 0 = 0$$
$$(u \times v) \cdot v = 6(0) + (-3)(1) + 1(3) = 0 - 3 + 3 = 0$$

3. Cross product $v \times u$:
$$v \times u = (1\cdot 0 - 3\cdot 2,\, 3\cdot 1 - 0\cdot 0,\, 0\cdot 2 - 1\cdot 1) = (-6, 3, -1)$$

Comparison: $v \times u = -(u \times v)$. Reversing the product order negates the resulting vector, reversing its spatial direction.
