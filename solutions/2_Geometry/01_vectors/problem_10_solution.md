## Question

The points $A=(0,0,0)$, $B=(2,1,0)$, and $C=(1,3,2)$ form a triangle. Construct $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$, compute the area of the parallelogram using the cross product, and then find the area of the triangle. Explain the factor $\frac12$.

## Solution

1. Vectors:
$$\overrightarrow{AB} = (2,1,0),\qquad \overrightarrow{AC} = (1,3,2)$$

2. Parallelogram area:
$$\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (1\cdot 2 - 0\cdot 3,\, 0\cdot 1 - 2\cdot 2,\, 2\cdot 3 - 1\cdot 1) = (2, -4, 5)$$
$$\text{Area}_{\text{para}} = \|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}\| = \sqrt{2^2 + (-4)^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{4 + 16 + 25} = \sqrt{45} = 3\sqrt{5}$$

3. Triangle area:
$$\text{Area}_{\text{tri}} = \frac{1}{2} \text{Area}_{\text{para}} = \frac{3\sqrt{5}}{2}$$

4. The factor $\frac{1}{2}$ arises because a triangle formed by two vectors spans exactly half the area of the parallelogram formed by the same vectors.
