# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

## Problem Statement

Given

$$A= \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ \end{pmatrix},\qquad B= \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -2 & 3 \\ 4 & 1 \\ \end{pmatrix}.$$

1. State the sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$.
2. Read off the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$.
3. Write the second row of $A$ and the first column of $B$ as vectors.

---

## Solution

### Part 1. Sizes of Matrices

The size of a matrix is written as $m \times n$, where $m$ represents the number of rows and $n$ represents the number of columns.

- Matrix $A$ has $2$ rows and $3$ columns, so its size is $2 \times 3$.
- Matrix $B$ has $3$ rows and $2$ columns, so its size is $3 \times 2$.

### Part 2. Reading Specific Entries

An entry $a_{ij}$ denotes the element in row $i$ and column $j$.

- $a_{12}$ is the entry in the $1^{\mathrm{st}}$ row and $2^{\mathrm{nd}}$ column of $A$:

$$a_{12} = -1$$

- $a_{23}$ is the entry in the $2^{\mathrm{nd}}$ row and $3^{\mathrm{rd}}$ column of $A$:

$$a_{23} = 5$$

- $b_{21}$ is the entry in the $2^{\mathrm{nd}}$ row and $1^{\mathrm{st}}$ column of $B$:

$$b_{21} = -2$$

- $b_{32}$ is the entry in the $3^{\mathrm{rd}}$ row and $2^{\mathrm{nd}}$ column of $B$:

$$b_{32} = 1$$

### Part 3. Rows and Columns as Vectors

The second row of matrix $A$ written as a row vector:

$$r_2(A) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \\ \end{pmatrix}$$

The first column of matrix $B$ written as a column vector:

$$c_1(B) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \\ \end{pmatrix}$$
